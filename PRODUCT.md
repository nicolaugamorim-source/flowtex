# Flowtex – Product Definition

## Users

- **Primary:** Founders and solopreneurs building SaaS/startups
- **Secondary:** CTOs, product leads, solo technical founders
- **Pain Point:** Context fragmentation across 10+ tools; constant context reset when using Claude/GPT

## Product Purpose

Flowtex is an AI second brain that unifies fragmented business context. It connects all user tools (Notion, Gmail, GitHub, Slack, Linear, Google Calendar, Stripe, Figma) and creates a unified knowledge base. When users ask Claude or other AI, Flowtex injects full business context automatically.

**Core Value:** "Stop explaining your business from scratch every time you open Claude. Flowtex remembers everything."

## Brand Voice

- Technical but warm (not cold SaaS speak)
- Direct and concrete (no "elevate," "seamless," "unleash")
- Founder-to-founder tone
- Confidence without arrogance

## Anti-References

- Overly corporate tone (Salesforce, Workday vibe)
- Crypto/blockchain aesthetic
- Cheap startup clichés (neon, "disrupt," generic SaaS design)
- Generic AI purple/blue gradients

## Strategic Principles

1. **Premium positioning:** This is a high-end, discerning founder's tool. Design should feel like a $10M product.
2. **Technical credibility:** Founders are smart. Show depth, not simplicity hiding complexity.
3. **Context-driven:** Every design decision must serve the core value prop: unified, available context.
4. **Minimal friction:** Fast signup, clear value, no BS.

## Register

**Brand** – This is a marketing landing page. Design IS the product presentation.

## Design Direction

**Light Mode Premium** – Clean, accessible, modern premium aesthetic. Teal accent (#00D4A4) for differentiation and energy. Rejects dark-mode-by-default startup clichés in favor of clarity and founder-friendly accessibility.

## Current Stage

Private beta, closing waitlist soon. Landing page is the only conversion funnel.
